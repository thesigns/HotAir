How to fly

Fly as far from the launch field as the propane allows, then land softly on dry, flat ground.

You only go up and down – every height has its own wind. The red pilot balloon shows the winds; the ladder on the right remembers them (green arrows take you further).

Burn early and wait: the balloon answers late. Score = metres × landing (exceptional ×1.5, good ×1, rough ×0.5). Water, buildings, power lines, other balloons and a hard touchdown are a crash.

Right thumb – BURN (hold) · left thumb – VENT (hold; on the ground it deflates the balloon) · drag to look around, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset the view.

W / ↑ / Space – burn · S / ↓ – vent · mouse: drag, wheel, double-click · P – pause
